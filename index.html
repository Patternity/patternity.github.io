Patternity

Patternity is a research organization focused on the design of protocol-level systems for behavioral consensus and off-chain verification.

Proof-of-Game (PoG)

Proof-of-Game (PoG) is a consensus protocol for proving the uniqueness of behavioral processes.

PoG does not prove:

  • human identity
  • resistance to automation or AI
  • skill, performance, or outcomes
  • economic value or incentives

Status: PoG v1.0 — Draft (consensus-complete)

Implementation: Uniqueness Engine (UE) not yet published
