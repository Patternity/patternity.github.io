Patternity

Protocol-level systems for signals in interactive media.

Patternity is a research organization focused on building protocol-level primitives for extracting, transporting, and verifying signals produced by interactive systems and media.

GhostSignal New

A protocol for embedding and extracting structured data directly from media streams such as video (and later audio).

Use cases: referral and advertising links carried by the content, coupons/claims bound to an intact media artifact, privacy-first attribution, and platform-independent conversion triggers.

Status: GhostSignal v0 — Draft

Demo: Transport-layer prototype available (browser-based)

Proof-of-Game (PoG)

Proof-of-Game (PoG) is a consensus protocol for proving the uniqueness of behavioral processes.

PoG does not attempt to prove identity, resistance to automation, skill, or outcomes.

Status: PoG v1.0 — Draft (consensus-complete)

Implementation: Uniqueness Engine (UE) not yet published
